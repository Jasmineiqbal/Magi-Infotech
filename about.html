About us


Magi Infotech Solution


Magi Infotech Solution provides professional document accessibility remediation services to help organizations comply with WCAG and ADA accessibility requirements. We convert your digital documents into accessible formats that can be used by people with disabilities and are compatible with assistive technologies such as screen readers.

Standards We Follow

Our accessibility remediation follows internationally
recognized accessibility standards, including:

  • WCAG 2.1 Level AA
  • ADA (Americans with Disabilities Act)
  • PDF/UA
  • Section 508
  • Microsoft Accessibility Best Practices
  • Adobe Accessibility Guidelines

Why Choose Magi Infotech Solution?


  • Experienced accessibility specialists
  • Fast turnaround time
  • High-quality remediation
  • Screen reader compatibility testing
  • Manual quality assurance
  • Affordable pricing
  • Secure handling of confidential documents

Our Process

1

Receive Your Source Files

Share your PDF, Word, PowerPoint, InDesign, or other source documents securely with our team.

2

Accessibility Assessment

We analyze your document to identify issues with headings, reading order, tables, images, forms, and color contrast.

3

Document Remediation

Our specialists remediate the document to meet WCAG 2.1 AA, PDF/UA, ADA, and Section 508 standards.

4

Quality Assurance & Testing

Every document is validated using accessibility tools and manually tested with screen readers.

5

Final Delivery

Receive fully accessible, standards-compliant documents that are ready for publishing and distribution.
